About This Guide

This brand guidelines document establishes the visual and verbal standards for Riverside Fairways. It ensures consistent, professional brand representation across all channels—digital, print, social media, and in-person interactions.

1. Brand Overview

Mission

Riverside Fairways brings the fairway to you. We are a fully mobile golf simulator company that delivers premium entertainment experiences to corporate events, celebrations, weddings, and parties throughout Louisiana and beyond.

Brand Essence

Mobile • Sophisticated • Accessible

Riverside Fairways combines luxury golf experience with the convenience of mobility. We're sophisticated without being stuffy—approachable without being casual.

Brand Promise

"We bring the fairway to you" — Premium golf entertainment, anywhere, anytime.

Core Brand Values

  • Mobile-First: We set up anywhere with electricity
  • Luxury: Premium experience, professional service
  • Accessible: No golf experience required, fun for all skill levels
  • Sophisticated: Elegant, quality-focused experience
  • Community-Oriented: Perfect for corporate team building and celebrations

2. Visual Identity: Logo

Logo Mark

The Riverside Fairways logo features an elegant script wordmark with an integrated golf flag icon. The design conveys sophistication through graceful letterforms and subtle movement.

Logo Variations

  • Horizontal Lockup: Preferred for most uses. Includes tagline "BRINGING THE FAIRWAY TO YOU"
  • Icon Only: Golf flag in isolation. Use for social avatars, apps, favicons. Minimum: 120px digital, 0.5" print.
  • Monochrome (Black): Use on white or light backgrounds.
  • Reversed (White): Use on Primary Green or Dark Green backgrounds.

Clear Space & Sizing

  • Clear Space: Equal to golf flag height on all sides
  • Minimum Size (Digital): 120 pixels wide
  • Minimum Size (Print): 0.75 inches wide

Logo Don'ts

  • ❌ Never stretch or distort
  • ❌ Never change colors
  • ❌ Never add effects (shadows, gradients)
  • ❌ Never place on busy backgrounds
  • ❌ Never rotate (except 180°)

3. Color Palette

Primary Colors

Primary Green

  • HEX: #5A8F7B
  • RGB: 90, 143, 123
  • CMYK: 37, 0, 14, 44
  • Pantone: 16-1546 TCX
  • Usage: Primary brand color for logos, headers, buttons

Secondary Ivory

  • HEX: #F5F1EB
  • RGB: 245, 241, 235
  • CMYK: 0, 2, 4, 4
  • Pantone: 12-0605 TCX
  • Usage: Background color for pages and sections

Accent & Supporting Colors

  • Light Green (#7BA691): Hover states, accents
  • Dark Green (#3D5D52): Text, visual weight
  • Warm Neutral (#D4C5B9): Decorative accents
  • Charcoal (#333333): Primary text
  • White (#FFFFFF): Primary background
Accessibility: All color combinations meet WCAG AA standards for contrast. Primary Green on Ivory (5.2:1) and Dark Green on White (7.1:1) are fully accessible.

4. Typography

Primary Typeface: Segoe UI / Helvetica Neue

  • Usage: Headlines, body text, UI elements
  • Weights: Regular (400), Semibold (600), Bold (700)

Type Hierarchy

  • Heading 1 (H1): 42px, Light (300), letter-spacing 2px — Page titles
  • Heading 2 (H2): 28px, Semibold (600), letter-spacing 1px — Section headers
  • Body Text: 16px, Regular (400), line-height 1.6 — Paragraph content
  • Small Text: 12px, Regular, #999999 — Captions, metadata

5. Brand Voice & Tone

Voice Characteristics

  • Friendly / Approachable: Conversational and personable, avoids jargon
  • Luxury / Sophisticated: Professional and polished, confident
  • Casual / Conversational: Uses contractions, direct address

Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

"Riverside Fairways brings championship-quality golf simulation to your event. Fully mobile and set up anywhere with electricity, our premium simulator creates unforgettable moments for corporate events, weddings, and celebrations."

Primary Messages

  • Fully Mobile: Set up anywhere, no golf course required
  • Premium Experience: Championship-quality, professional service
  • Perfect for Any Event: Corporate, weddings, celebrations
  • Accessible: No golf experience required, fun for all

6. Brand Applications

Digital

  • Website: Logo on header, Primary Green buttons, Charcoal body text
  • Email: Logo header, green CTA buttons, signature accent bar
  • Social Media: Primary Green profile color, white text with dark shadow
  • Mobile App: Dark Green status bar, Primary Green navigation

Print

  • Business Cards: Logo left, white background, Primary Green border accent
  • Brochures: Hero photo with green overlay, Ivory backgrounds
  • Signage: Logo min 2" wide, high contrast, maintain clear space
  • Promotional: Branded Primary Green, prominent logo and CTA
