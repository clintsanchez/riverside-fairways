How Booking Works

We keep our own calendar rather than letting dates get claimed automatically. That's what stops two events landing on the same day.

Request your date

Send the date, the venue and roughly how many guests. Takes two minutes.

We confirm & quote

{{custom_values.owner_first_name}} checks the calendar, confirms your venue works, and sends a quote and agreement.

Sign, deposit, done

Your date is reserved once the agreement is signed and the deposit is in. Then we show up ready to go.

ⓘ

Worth knowing: asking about a date doesn't hold it, and neither does a quote. Until the agreement is signed and the deposit is paid, the day stays open. If you're set on a specific Saturday, that's the reason not to sit on the paperwork.
