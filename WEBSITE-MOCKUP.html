FAQ Common Questions

How much space do you need to set up? +
We need approximately 20x15 feet of clear space with access to a standard electrical outlet. That's about the size of a large conference room. No golf course required!
Do I need WiFi for the simulator to work? +
WiFi is not required, but it's helpful for scoring and leaderboard features. We can work without it, though. Our equipment is fully mobile and self-contained.
How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks ahead for regular packages and 4-6 weeks for large events. We do accept last-minute bookings subject to availability.
What's included in the Feral Package? +
The Feral Package includes our golf simulator plus 14 additional games (arcade-style, competitive games perfect for kids and casual players), music setup, professional MC, and full-day booking for maximum fun.
Do you offer coaching or instruction? +
Yes! Our Premium and Feral packages include professional coaching and instruction. We can help beginners get started or provide tips for experienced golfers.
