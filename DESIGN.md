## Brand
- **Name:** Riverside Fairways
- **Contact:** Christy Browning — jase@riversidefairways.com — +12259782363
- **What they do:** We are a mobile golf simulator; great for corporate events, casual events, weddings, parties, etc. We also offer a “Feral” package that includes a variety of 14 additional simulator games; greats for kids parties.
- **Differentiator:** We are fully mobile and can set up anywhere we have access to electricity (access to wifi is a plus but not required).
- **Audience:** For our regular package, 25-55 year olds that want to have a fun time hitting the virtual links and mid to large local corporations (think plants, engineering firms, etc). For the feral package I would expect to see more kids aged 5-12 or teens/adults that just want to have a more relaxed fun vibe going for their event.
- **Voice:** Friendly / Approachable, Luxury / Sophisticated, Casual / Conversational
