When working client feedback (markup.io comments, texts), change **only** what
was asked. Adjacent cleanup that looks obviously correct is not welcome.

Two corrections from Clint:

- **"did she ask for that?"** — Christy asked to remove a Membership link and fix
  dead links in the footer. I also renamed Homepage→Home, dropped the "Pages"
  column and added Pricing/Resources/Swag. None of that was requested.
- **"leave the social icons"** — I *removed* hrefless team-card social icons.
  She wanted them to **work**, not to go away. Restored and linked.

**Why:** the client reviews against what she asked for. Extra changes read as
unrequested churn, cost her review time, and make it unclear what was actually
addressed — and a "fix" can invert her intent, as the social icons did.

**How to apply:** make the literal change. If adjacent work looks warranted,
finish the ask first, then raise it as a separate offer and let Clint decide.

rather than tidying them, and said so.
